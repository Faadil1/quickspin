## Capture gate

A take is not accepted unless it visibly contains:

- canonical memory sentence;
- classic exact 12-second control;
- QuickSpin same-wait path;
- at least one execution signal becoming gameplay;
- truthful Wait Receipt;
- private Evidence Capsule;
- Wait Ghost privacy reduction labels;
- historical Ghost replay clearly labeled not live AI;
- wait diff with no winner/trust score if included;
- failed negative path with no fabricated answer;
- UNKNOWN/provenance rule;
- final runtime/repo identity.

## Claim boundaries

- Do not call the controlled 12-second run live production AI.
- Do not call the controlled rejected Promise a live provider outage.
- Do not claim QuickSpin reduces provider/model latency.
- Do not claim every user perceives a shorter wait.
- Do not call an Evidence Capsule or Wait Ghost cryptographically signed unless a real signing primitive is added and verified.
- Do not describe Wait Ghost replay as live AI.
- Do not describe Compare as deciding which wait is “better.”
- Do not claim the public competitor scan is exhaustive.

## Editorial rules

- **value before technology**;
- one narrative, not a feature montage;
- show proof before plumbing;
- keep failure/UNKNOWN visible rather than sanitizing the demo;
